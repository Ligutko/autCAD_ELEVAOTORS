    Pit for grain unloading – a technological, reliable, and fully mechanized structure that allows for quick and spill-free unloading of grain from the rear and side compartments of vehicles.

    The pit is covered with drivable grates made of galvanized steel, which can withstand the load from grain-loaded vehicles. The drivable grates are installed on cross beams that are attached to the foundation of the pit during the construction phase.

    The bunker is made of galvanized sheet material with bolted joints.

    Characteristics of the pit for unloading:

    • The capacity of the pit is 40 m3, 55 m3, and 77 m3.
    • The length of the drivable area is 6,640 mm, 9,370 mm, and 12,100 mm respectively.
    • The width of the drivable area is 4,000 mm.

    The pit can be equipped with chain conveyors with a capacity ranging from 50 to 250 tons per hour.
