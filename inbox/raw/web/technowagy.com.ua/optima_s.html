Truck scales TBA… “Optima-S” designed and manufactured by “Technowagy” Ltd are intended for static weighing of trucks.

Scales with a length of the platform from 6 to 24 m, with a maximum weighing range of 30-80 tons and an axle load of 15 tons, designed for medium usage conditions.

Advantages of “Optima-S” scales manufactured by “Technowagy” Ltd:

1. A unique patented construction of scales ensures:

• the scales comes out of our plant with the Approval of the State Standard – customer doesn’t need to call a Approval officer with weight samples;

• the scales comes out of our plant as a finished product, in the form of modules that are easily assembled or disassembled for installation elsewhere;

• quick mounting of the weight – 3-4 hours;

• digital mass measurement system;

• maximum protection from third-party intervention in scales indications;

• automatic diagnostics of each measurement channel in case of emergency situations;

• the scales design does not require regulation in the process of exploitation.

2. The modular design of the platform has the following advantages:

thermocompensation of platform, which according to the laws of physics at low temperatures (in

winter) tapers and at high (summer) expands. The presence of gaps compensates for these

phenomena and the platform does not create additional negative effects on very sensitive electronic

load cells. Solid welded construction does not provide this.The platform better copies the relief of the foundation, as a result of which the load on the sensors is distributed evenly – the readings are more accurate.

There are no welding places in load-bearing structures – there are no potential places for the

appearance of cracks. Simple and easy cleaning of the space under the platform.

3. Our enterprise doesn’t save on metal for weighbridge – we use only new metal (most of which

we import from Europe) on the assumption that the exploit conditions are going to be intense and

longstanding.

4. The roadway covered with checked steel plates 8mm.

5. The height of the platform is 350 mm, which reduces the length of the ramp and ensures

convenient upgrade for a vehicle.

6. The scales overload capacity is more than 200%.

Load cells RC3 of company “Flintec” (Germany) are made of stainless steel

Indicator TWP-25 manufactured by Technovagy Ltd.

The scales are mounted on the airfield or road plates, solid foundation, by surface or half-surface

technology. Building works in the part of the scales foundation can be done by a Consumer or by

our specialists according to the drafts we provide.

Regulations:

1.“Technowagy” Ltd. passed through the procedure for conformity assessment of scales for modul D (type compliance by ensuring the quality of the production process), which means high confidence of the state metrological service in the competence of our company’s employees, modern metrological equipment of production units and excellent product quality. We received the right to produce, test and control products, apply appropriate labeling and issue a declaration of compliance on the basis of internal quality system procedures, eliminating the need for additional control of our weighing equipment by representatives of state metrological centers.

2. Type examination certificate for truck scales TBA.

3. License for construction work № АВ 601026 – we can provide the “turnkey” scales together with

construction works.

4. Certificate of conformity of the quality management system – ISO 9001: 2015.

Scales  “Technowagy” Ltd do not require paid maintenance during operation.
