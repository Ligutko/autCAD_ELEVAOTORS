Recommended Temperature Cable Placement

The amount and length of the temperature cables to efficiently monitor the grain temperature depends on the bin dimensions (diameter, eaves height and peak height). Many bin manufactures provide brackets for temperature cables in fixed positions in their roof assembly. Other customers prefer an individual setup. We are able to due both.
